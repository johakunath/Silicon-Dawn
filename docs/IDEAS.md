## Anno-style colony (big pivot), 2026-11-23
Show the colonists on the map: settlements with needs tiers (power, connectivity, AI services) that grow when served and pay taxes or fees, like Anno's population tiers. Infrastructure would serve visible towns instead of an abstract market. Pros: very tangible demand, strong city-builder fun. Cons: changes the genre, large art and simulation work, overlaps with the off-map market in E3. Decide after the market (E3) is playable.
